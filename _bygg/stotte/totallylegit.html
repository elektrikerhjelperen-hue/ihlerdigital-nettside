FAQ

My purchase didn't arrive.

Open the Shop and tap "Restore purchases". Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel Totally Legit Pro?

Settings on your iPhone > [your name] > Subscriptions > Totally Legit Pro > Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I want a refund.

Refunds are handled by Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

Turn on iCloud for the game on both devices and sign in with the same Apple ID. Your progress syncs automatically.

How do I turn off personalised ads?

Open Settings in the game and change your ad privacy choice, or turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Totally Legit Pro removes all ads.

Is this game teaching scams?

No. It's a cartoon comedy about silly weasels. Real scams hurt real people – never give strangers access to your computer or bank account.
