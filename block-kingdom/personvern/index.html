Privacy Policy - Block Kingdom

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Block Kingdom is a puzzle game for iPhone. This policy explains what data the app uses.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, currency, settings and purchase status are stored locally on your device. We do not run a server that receives this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, if you allow tracking), IP address and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice in Settings inside the app. On iOS you can allow or deny tracking in the system prompt. More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, scores may be shared with Apple Game Center according to Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow them, the app schedules local reminder notifications on your device. No data is sent to us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, delete or restrict the use of your data. Because we do not store personal data on our servers, requests about advertising data are handled by Google: https://myadcenter.google.com
